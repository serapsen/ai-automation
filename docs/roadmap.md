# One-Slide Roadmap

- **Epic 1 – Architecture Setup (1 day)**
  - Mermaid diagrams, repo scaffolding, environment, logging.
  - Roles: Creative Lead (sign-off), AdOps (requirements), IT (env), Legal (review).

- **Epic 2 – Pipeline Development (3 days)**
  - Ingestion, GenAI generation with fallback, post-processing, outputs.
  - Roles: Creative Lead (briefs), AdOps (asset org), IT (APIs), Legal (brand compliance).

- **Epic 3 – Agent & Reporting (2 days)**
  - Agent monitor, counting/alerts, stakeholder communications.
  - Roles: Creative Lead (acceptance), AdOps (monitoring), IT (ops), Legal (approvals).
